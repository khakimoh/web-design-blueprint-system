# playground/src/app/features/spacing/

Spacing page: the 4 px spacing scale, radius scale, elevation shadows, control heights, z-index
layers and motion durations (hover or focus a row to play it; reduced motion sets them to 0 ms).

- Files: `spacing-page.ts/.html/.scss`
- Data source: `--ds-space-*`, `--ds-radius-*`, `--ds-shadow-*`, `--ds-control-height-*`, `--ds-z-*`, `--ds-duration-*`, `--ds-easing-*`.
