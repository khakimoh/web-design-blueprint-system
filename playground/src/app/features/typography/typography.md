# playground/src/app/features/typography/

Typography page: every `.ds-type-*` role rendered, English and Persian samples (showing that
tracking and uppercase are reset for Persian), the type scale, families, weights and line heights.

- Files: `typography-page.ts/.html/.scss`
- Data source: `.ds-type-*` classes and `--ds-font-*` / `--ds-line-height-*` custom properties.
